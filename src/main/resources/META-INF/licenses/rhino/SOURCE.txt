Oriel includes Mozilla Rhino, relocated to dev.oriel.shadow.rhino and modified.

Rhino is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (LICENSE.txt); NOTICE.txt
lists the other code it contains. The modifications are the patch series in
rhino/patches of the Oriel source tree (fresh let/const bindings per loop
iteration, spread arguments, class syntax, async functions and await, a size
limit on BigInt arithmetic and parsing, and no CommonJS modules), applied to the
upstream source below. They stay under MPL-2.0.

Source: the upstream commit
https://github.com/mozilla/rhino/tree/8527ebbf6d0b1599f819b5001af06e5dd9147013
(Rhino 1.9.1). Obtain the Oriel source matching the distributed version,
including the patch series and the package rewrite, from the project repository.

The 1.20.1 Forge port compiles that patched source directly into the mod jar
instead of shadow-relocating a separate artifact: every org.mozilla.javascript
class was moved to dev.oriel.shadow.rhino and every org.mozilla.classfile class
to dev.oriel.shadow.rhino.classfile, the optimizer, serialize and commonjs
packages were dropped, and only Messages.properties and Messages_en.properties
are shipped.
